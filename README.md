<h1>Simple Rename v1.0</h1>
<p>Simple Rename is a lightweight and efficient desktop tool designed to help you batch rename files quickly and easily.</p>

<h2>Features</h2>
<ul>
    <li><strong>Drag & Drop:</strong> Simply drag and drop files directly into the application.</li>
    <li><strong>Prefix/Suffix:</strong> Easily add custom text to the beginning or end of your filenames.</li>
    <li><strong>Find & Replace:</strong> Quickly search for and replace specific characters or phrases.</li>
    <li><strong>Auto-Numbering:</strong> Supports automatic numbering with configurable start values, padding, and positioning.</li>
    <li><strong>User-Friendly Interface:</strong> A clean, modern, and intuitive design.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How to Use</h2>
<ol>
    <li>Select files by dragging and dropping or clicking the <strong>Browse</strong> button.</li>
    <li>Configure your desired options (Prefix, Suffix, Find/Replace, or Numbering).</li>
    <li>Click the <strong>Rename</strong> button to apply changes.</li>
</ol>
